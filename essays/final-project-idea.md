# First Idea, Music Sharing Website

*La la laaaaaa, laaa la la*

# Overview

Problem: UH Manoa has many students with many different music tastes. Students at UH Manoa have no real way to see what’s popular amongst the student community. 

Solution: Our application, Bowz Bops, can help students at UH Manoa find out what students are listening to and popular playlists for different activities like studying or going to the beach. Users will be able to create playlists of their favorite songs and submit their playlists to popular categories, like study, party, beach and more. Users can like their favorite playlists, and the most popular playlists can be listed for all users per category.

# Approach

Following similar formats to digits and other ICS 314 experiences, our application will utilize user profiles and personally added information. Users will be able to enter in favorite songs and group them into playlists, similar to how the list stuff works in the digits ICS 314 experience. Users will be able to edit information, and share their playlists with other users who will be their friends. Users can view their friends playlists and add likes, displaying how popular their playlists are. The relevant input fields would be the name of the song, artist, year, and genre at the least, and maybe also including a link to either Spotify or Youtube for users to listen to the song from the website.

# Use Case Ideas

Our application will enable students and other users to be able to showcase what tunes and tastes they have and have the ability to enjoy the music tastes of other users and their friends. The application will have preset categories for users to submit their playlists to, examples like: playlists for studying, partying, beach, workout, and have the application display the most liked playlists per genre. This will give students a dynamically updating social media platform that will give them the perfect songs to listen to as the school year progresses.

# Beyond the Basics

To separate our application from the simpler ICS 314 experiences, like digits, our website will contain more features for users to share their curated lists. Advanced features include the ability to like other playlists and have the most popular playlist per category featured on a leaderboard. Additionally have the ability to embed Youtube links to listen to the music video without having to search for the song outside of the application.

# Next Idea, Study Space Finder

*Zzzzzzzzzzzzzzzzzzz*

# Overview

Problem: Having a gap between classes can be useful, but figuring out where to spend that time can definitely be frustrating, especially in a large campus like UH Manoa. A student may need a quiet place to charge their laptop, or a quiet place outside to enjoy the sun while studying, or a place close to where you can grab a quick meal and eat while studying.

Solution: We would create an online application where students could login, review suggested study spots on campus, rate those study spots, or add study spots. Each study spot would have a description of it, as well as the benefits and cons of each one. It would have a rating of noise level, comfortability, hours open, and a short description of each spot where students would review. It would include an admin page where an admin could review different spots and add it to the application or delete ones as well.

# Approach

#### Mockup page ideas:
Landing page
Browsing location page
User home page
Location Details page
My profile and favorites page
Request a location page
Admin page

# Use Case Ideas

### Sharing a Useful location:
A student finds a useful spot that is missing from the application and they can go on the application and add their new favorite spot.

### Discovering a Useful Alternative
If a usual spot is full or crowded, a student would be able to open their personalized suggestions and find another place with similar features.

# Beyond the Basics

If we have time after completing the main features, we could add an interactive campus map so students can see where locations are in relation to their next class.

Another addition would be temporary crowd reports. Students could mark a location as quiet, moderately busy, or crowded. Each report would show when it was submitted and expire after a short period so older reports would not appear current.

We could also let students mark reviews as helpful. This would make useful details, such as where outlets are located or when a space tends to get noisy, easier to find. Our first priority would be getting browsing, filtering, favorites, preferences, and location requests working before adding these extra features.
