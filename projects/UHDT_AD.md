For the University of Hawaii Drone Technologies Verticaly Integrated project, we were performing a payload delivery mission. The mission requirement was to have an autonomous drone capable of target recognition and controlled delivery of a payload within 5 feet of the target. At the time, I was a member of the Air Delivery subsystem responsible for housing and deploying the payload, a little water bottle attached to a string. One of our major design constraints was to have the parts be modular and easy to replace with spare parts.

My design took advantage of an unused platform on the bottom of the drone, screwing in securely. This design was fully symmetrical and modular, allowing all four corners to be replaced with the same exact part. This design was not used in the final product however due to concerns the housing would be in the way of the camera gimbal that is on the side of the drone.
